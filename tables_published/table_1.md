|---|---|

*Note.* Derivation cohort of the public multimodal polysomnography dataset of Tao et al. (2025); all counts are read from the frozen analysis signature. Analysis unit: non-overlapping 30-s window; independent sampling unit: patient (n = 50). Positive = window overlaps a retained, non-wake, expert-scored respiratory event by ≥ 10 s; indeterminate = non-zero overlap shorter than 10 s (excluded). The 10-s overlap threshold is a research operational definition anchored to the AASM minimum event duration, not a clinically validated window-classification threshold. The airflow sub-cohort (34 patients) is used only for the secondary paired analysis (Table 4). Audio was available in the source but not used. AASM = American Academy of Sleep Medicine.
